About

Background & research focus

Stallone Msamba (MBA, PGDip, fCMgr) is a Malawian business and technology professional and independent researcher currently based in South Africa. His work brings together digital transformation, artificial intelligence, business strategy, project coordination, operations management, and technology-enabled development across Africa.

He has experience evaluating AI-generated text, images, audio, and video; checking outputs for accuracy and consistency; annotating training data; and providing structured feedback against quality guidelines. His management background includes construction project coordination, budgeting and cost tracking, supplier coordination, staff supervision, operational performance, financial reconciliations, and customer service.

His research explores how organisations, SMEs, public institutions, and communities can adopt digital technologies to improve productivity, inclusion, service delivery, and sustainable development. Key interests include Afrocentric digital frameworks, AI adoption, digital agriculture, digital entrepreneurship, and digital ecosystems in emerging economies.
